# Item 8: SSR is not expensive

Source: [https://t3.gg/blog/post/ssr-is-not-expensive](https://t3.gg/blog/post/ssr-is-not-expensive)

Summary:
This post argues that server-side rendering (SSR) is not a significant cost driver and often results in lower overall cloud expenses compared to client-side rendering. It details how SSR's overhead is minimal, can reduce other infrastructure costs by consolidating requests, and improves user experience with faster, more contextual rendering. The article encourages SSR adoption for anything beyond trivial static sites.

Key takeaways:
- SSR's computational cost is minor compared to database/auth overhead.
- SSR consolidates requests, reducing infrastructure load and costs.
- Enhances user experience with faster, more meaningful initial renders.
- Supports modern strategies like caching, streaming, and selective hydration.
