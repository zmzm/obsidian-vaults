# Item 4: Stack Overflow Dev Survey - The 2024 results

Source: [https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/)

Summary:
The 2024 Stack Overflow Developer Survey provides insights into developer demographics, learning habits, tool preferences, and attitudes toward AI. It highlights trends in database usage, programming languages, AI tool adoption, and work environments.

Key takeaways:
- PostgreSQL is now the most popular database; JavaScript, Python, and SQL remain top languages.
- 76% of respondents use or plan to use AI tools in development; most do not see AI as a job threat.
- Svelte and ChatGPT are highly admired among frameworks and AI tools, respectively.
- Most developers rely on online resources and API documentation for learning.
