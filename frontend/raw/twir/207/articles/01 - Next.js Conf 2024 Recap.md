# Item 1: Next.js Conf 2024 Recap

Source: [https://vercel.com/blog/recap-next-js-conf-2024](https://vercel.com/blog/recap-next-js-conf-2024)

Summary:
The recap covers major announcements from Next.js Conf 2024, including five new Next.js versions, over 4,000 merged pull requests, and a growing contributor base. Key highlights are the stable release of Next.js 15, improvements to the App Router and caching semantics, and Turbopack Dev's stability. The conference emphasized progressive disclosure of complexity, making Next.js approachable for all project sizes, and introduced new resources for self-hosting and community engagement. All ticket proceeds were donated to open source, and recorded sessions are available online.

Key takeaways:
- Next.js 15 is stable, with significant improvements to caching, App Router, and developer experience.
- Turbopack Dev is now stable, offering up to 90% faster code updates; focus is shifting to Turbopack for builds.
- Progressive disclosure of complexity aims to make Next.js easier for both beginners and advanced users.
- New community GitHub org and resources for self-hosting; ticket proceeds support open source.
