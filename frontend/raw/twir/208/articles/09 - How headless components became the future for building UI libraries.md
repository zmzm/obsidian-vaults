# Item 9: How headless components became the future for building UI libraries

Source: [https://www.subframe.com/blog/how-headless-components-became-the-future-for-building-ui-libraries](https://www.subframe.com/blog/how-headless-components-became-the-future-for-building-ui-libraries)

Summary:
Headless component libraries, like Radix and HeadlessUI, have become the standard for React UI development due to their flexibility and ease of styling. This article traces the rise of headless components, their advantages over pre-styled libraries, and the ecosystem's evolution with tools like Shadcn and Subframe. The trend is expected to continue, with more libraries and tooling emerging to support custom design systems and code-first workflows.

Key takeaways:
- Headless components provide unstyled, accessible building blocks, making custom theming easy.
- Radix and Shadcn have driven adoption by simplifying design system scaffolding.
- The community is shifting away from pre-styled libraries due to styling challenges.
- The headless approach is influencing new tools, startups, and even established libraries.
