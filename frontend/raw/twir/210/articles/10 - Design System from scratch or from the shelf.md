# Item 10: Design System: from scratch or from the shelf?

Source: [https://www.voorhoede.nl/en/blog/design-system-from-scratch-from-the-shelf/](https://www.voorhoede.nl/en/blog/design-system-from-scratch-from-the-shelf/)

Summary:
The post compares building a custom design system from scratch versus adopting an off-the-shelf component library, weighing factors like flexibility, maintenance, and cost. It discusses styled vs. unstyled (headless) libraries, provides a comparison matrix of popular options, and emphasizes the importance of proof-of-concept testing before committing. The article also shares a real-world example of customizing Vuetify for a client.

Key takeaways:
- Off-the-shelf libraries save time but may limit design flexibility and control.
- Headless (unstyled) libraries offer more customization at the cost of more work.
- Comparison matrices help evaluate library fit for project needs.
- Proof-of-concept is recommended before full adoption.
