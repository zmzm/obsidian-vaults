# React Foundation Meetup: London

​Join the [React Foundation](https://react.foundation/?utm_source=luma) in London for an evening with the React & React Native Community!

​Whether you’re building your first app or have been working with it for years, come meet fellow developers, exchange ideas, and share what you’re working on. It’s a chance to put faces to familiar names, make new connections, and talk about the things you’re excited to build.  
  
You’ll also get to meet and hear from folks at our partner companies: [Meta](https://www.meta.com/?utm_source=luma), [Amazon](https://developer.amazon.com/?utm_source=luma), [Expo](https://expo.dev/home?utm_source=luma), [Software Mansion](https://swmansion.com/?utm_source=luma), [Callstack](https://www.callstack.com/?utm_source=luma), [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us?utm_source=luma), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/?utm_source=luma), and [Huawei](https://consumer.huawei.com/?utm_source=luma).

## ​Agenda highlights

​Bring your questions, your curiosity, and your love for React & React Native. Everyone is welcome.

​See you in London!
