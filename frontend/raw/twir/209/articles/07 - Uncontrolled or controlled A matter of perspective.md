# Item 7: Uncontrolled or controlled: A matter of perspective

Source: [https://buildui.com/posts/uncontrolled-vs-controlled-a-matter-of-perspective](https://buildui.com/posts/uncontrolled-vs-controlled-a-matter-of-perspective)

Summary:
This article explores the distinction between controlled and uncontrolled components in React, using input fields and counters as examples. It demonstrates that whether a component is controlled or uncontrolled depends not just on its internal state, but also on how it is used by its parent. Components can be designed to support both modes, with the controlling context determined by props and usage patterns.

Key takeaways:
- Controlled components have their state managed by React (via props), while uncontrolled components manage their own state.
- The same component can be controlled or uncontrolled depending on usage.
- Designing components to support both modes increases flexibility.
- The distinction is contextual: it's about how the component is used, not just its implementation.
