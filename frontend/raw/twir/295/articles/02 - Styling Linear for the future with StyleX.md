# Item 2: Styling Linear for the future with StyleX

Source: [https://linear.app/now/styling-linear-for-the-future-stylex](https://linear.app/now/styling-linear-for-the-future-stylex)

Summary:
Linear describes its migration from styled-components to StyleX, detailing the challenges, motivations, and strategies for moving a large React codebase. The article covers technical and organizational aspects, including the need for better performance, stricter styling boundaries, and improved developer ergonomics, as well as the use of codemods, agents, and custom tooling to manage the transition.

Key takeaways:
- styled-components' flexibility became a liability at scale; StyleX enforces stricter, more predictable styling boundaries.
- Performance was a key driver, especially after React 18 and styled-components entering maintenance mode.
- Migration was incremental, using codemods, agents, and custom lint/type-aware tools to minimize risk and enforce new conventions.
- Team adoption was encouraged through visible progress tracking and automated reminders.
