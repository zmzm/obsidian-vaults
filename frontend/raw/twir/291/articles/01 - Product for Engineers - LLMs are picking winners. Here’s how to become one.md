# Item 1: Product for Engineers - LLMs are picking winners. Here’s how to become one

Source: [https://go.posthog.com/twir-jul22](https://go.posthog.com/twir-jul22)

Summary:
This article discusses how large language models (LLMs) are increasingly influencing product discovery and recommendations, and introduces the concept of Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) as a way to improve your product's visibility in LLM-generated answers. It covers practical steps to assess and enhance your product's presence in LLMs, the continued relevance of traditional SEO, and the importance of honest measurement in AEO efforts. The author shares lessons from optimizing PostHog’s products for LLM visibility, emphasizing actionable strategies and the need for transparency in reporting results.

Key takeaways:
- LLMs drive significant traffic and conversions; being recommended by them is now critical for product growth.
- AEO builds on traditional SEO, but content must be structured for chunk-based retrieval and direct question answering.
- Focus first on improving your own content before chasing mentions on every external platform.
- Be skeptical of AEO metrics and case studies; prioritize honest, first-party data collection.
